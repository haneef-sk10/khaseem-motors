Privacy policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

This page explains how the Khaseem Motors website is intended to handle information. It must be reviewed by the showroom owner before the website is published.

1. Information collected by this website

This starter website does not include a contact form, account registration, advertising trackers or analytics code. It does not intentionally ask you to submit personal information directly through the website.

The hosting provider may process technical information, such as server requests and security logs, to operate and protect the website. The exact information and retention period depend on the hosting provider's current policies and settings.

2. WhatsApp and Google Maps

Links on this website can open WhatsApp or Google Maps. If you choose to use those services, the relevant provider may process information under its own privacy terms. Khaseem Motors does not control those providers' services or privacy practices.

3. Calls and messages

If you call or message the showroom, the phone number and any information you choose to share will be handled through the relevant phone or messaging service. Avoid sending information that is not needed for your enquiry.

4. Children's privacy

This website is intended to provide general showroom information. It is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children through website forms.

5. Changes to this policy

This policy should be updated if the website adds forms, analytics, advertising, customer accounts, online payments or other features that change how information is processed.

6. Contact

For questions about this website's privacy practices, contact Khaseem Motors at 95539 13245.
